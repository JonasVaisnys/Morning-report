Electricity market over the week

16–22 November · day-ahead market

 

Electricity cost 166.0 €/MWh on average over the week – 30% lower than the previous week (237.3 €/MWh).

The most expensive day was Thursday (229.1 €/MWh), the cheapest Saturday (52.7 €/MWh). The most expensive hour was 280.1 €/MWh (Thursday 09:00–10:00), the cheapest 15.9 €/MWh (Monday 00:00–01:00).

The price was pushed up most by expensive gas (+54 €/MWh per day on average): TTF ranged from 80.8 to 88.5 €/MWh during the week. It was pushed down by strong wind (−25 €/MWh): wind farms generated 971 MW on average, against 885 MW the previous week.

The interconnectors were fully loaded: NordBalt export to Sweden – 62 h, LitPol export to Poland – 93 h, LitPol import from Poland – 15 h, interconnectors importing from Latvia – 55 h (out of 168 h). Electricity in Lithuania was more expensive than in Latvia for 44 h, by 7.1 €/MWh on average over the week.

News of the week

The week's most important energy news · source given with each item

 

Top story

Vytenis Koryzna to lead Ignitis Group from January; D. Maikštėnas to leave office earlier

The Supervisory Council and the Management Board of Ignitis Group have given final approval to Management Board member Vytenis Koryzna as Chair of the Management Board and CEO from 1 January 2027. Darius Maikštėnas’s second and final term will end on 31 December 2026, rather than on 28 February 2027 as previously planned.

„Ignitis grupė“, 15min · 20 November

Lithuania

New head of Ignitis Group to take office earlier

The Supervisory Council and the Management Board of Ignitis Group have given final approval to Vytenis Koryzna as Chair of the Management Board and head of the group. His term will begin on 1 January 2027, and he will replace D. Maikštėnas earlier than planned.

Verslo žinios · 20 November

Regulator: easing of price growth depends on Ignitis and the state

The National Energy Regulatory Council (VERT) forecasts that from January regulated public electricity prices for consumers will be 23–27% higher and gas prices 38–46% higher. The head of the Council says he hears calls to soften the increase, but stresses that decisions on reducing tariffs are in the hands of Ignitis and the state.

Verslo žinios · 20 November

LNG terminal surplus to reduce gas supply security component

As the Klaipėda LNG terminal collected EUR 9.8 million more in regulated activity revenue than planned this year, the surplus will be returned to consumers next year. As a result, in 2027 the gas supply security component will fall to minus 55 EUR/MWh.

Verslo žinios · 19 November

Windfall tax on fuel producers and Orlen Lietuva reports

After the ruling coalition agreed to tax the windfall profits of fuel producers, the Deputy Minister of Finance says that drafting the bill would be considerably easier if Orlen Lietuva provided its financial statements. This oil refining company is considered the main potential payer of such a tax.

Verslo žinios · 17 November

Draft law on the Visaginas SEZ submitted to the Seimas

A draft law on the Visaginas SEZ has been submitted to the Seimas, aiming to strengthen the regional economy and to manage the consequences of dismantling nuclear infrastructure through economic measures. The draft also provides for reserving a territory next to the SEZ.

Verslo žinios · 21 November
