Electricity market

Thursday, 26 November · day-ahead market

 

Electricity today: 303.0 €/MWh – much more expensive than usual due to very expensive gas.

The price is pushed up most by very expensive gas (+100 €/MWh): TTF is at 88.1 €/MWh, 65% above its 12-month average. It is also pushed up by more expensive neighbouring markets (+35 €/MWh): Sweden (SE4) – 295.9, Poland – 302.7, Finland – 259.9 €/MWh.

For 17 hours the interconnectors from Latvia are fully loaded, so electricity in Lithuania is more expensive than in Latvia, by 36.7 €/MWh on average over the day.

The remaining part (“other”, −39 €/MWh) is not explained by these drivers. For almost the whole day the price is lower than the model expects: the Lithuanian price is close to the Swedish price (296 €/MWh) because the interconnector with Sweden is not fully loaded for most hours, so the price does not rise above the Swedish level.
