Electricity market

Friday, 27 November · day-ahead market

 

Electricity today: 322.9 €/MWh – much more expensive than usual due to very expensive gas.

The price is pushed up most by very expensive gas (+106 €/MWh): TTF is at 87.5 €/MWh, 63% above its 12-month average. It is also pushed up by more expensive neighbouring markets (+39 €/MWh): Sweden (SE4) – 317.1, Poland – 310.9, Finland – 204.1 €/MWh.

For 23 hours the interconnectors from Latvia are fully loaded, so electricity in Lithuania is more expensive than in Latvia, by 100.7 €/MWh on average over the day.

The remaining part (“other”, −16 €/MWh) is not explained by these drivers. For almost the whole day the price is lower than the model expects: the Lithuanian price is close to the Swedish price (317 €/MWh) because the interconnector with Sweden is not fully loaded for most hours, so the price does not rise above the Swedish level.
