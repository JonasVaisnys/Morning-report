Electricity market

Wednesday, 25 November · day-ahead market

 

Electricity today: 270.3 €/MWh – much more expensive than usual due to very expensive gas.

The price is pushed up most by very expensive gas (+94 €/MWh): TTF is at 90.4 €/MWh, 70% above its 12-month average. It is also pushed up by weak wind (+34 €/MWh): wind farms are generating only 166 MW, against about 502 MW normally at this time of year.

Exports to Poland use the full interconnector capacity for most of the day (LitPol – 151 of 205 MW): electricity in Poland costs 310.5 €/MWh (15:00–16:00 – 498.8 €/MWh). The fully loaded interconnector does not pass this price on to Lithuania, so neighbouring markets lower the price (−6 €/MWh). For 17 hours the interconnectors from Latvia are fully loaded, so electricity in Lithuania is more expensive than in Latvia, by 48.8 €/MWh on average over the day.

The remaining part (“other”, −19 €/MWh) is not explained by these drivers. For almost the whole day the price is lower than the model expects: the Lithuanian price is close to the Swedish price (284 €/MWh) because the interconnector with Sweden is not fully loaded for most hours, so the price does not rise above the Swedish level.
