Electricity market

Tuesday, 24 November · day-ahead market

 

Electricity today: 241.4 €/MWh – much more expensive than usual due to very expensive gas.

The price is pushed up most by very expensive gas (+86 €/MWh): TTF is at 91.4 €/MWh, 72% above its 12-month average. It is slightly pushed down by strong wind (−11 €/MWh): wind farms are generating 685 MW – 39% of consumption (1,772 MW); normal for the time of year is about 513 MW.

Exports to Poland use almost the full interconnector capacity (LitPol – 212 of 222 MW). For 11 hours the interconnectors from Latvia are fully loaded, so electricity in Lithuania is more expensive than in Latvia, by 31.3 €/MWh on average over the day.
