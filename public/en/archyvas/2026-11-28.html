Electricity market

Saturday, 28 November · Lithuanian bidding zone · day-ahead market

Electricity today: 221.3 €/MWh – much more expensive than usual due to very expensive gas.

The price is pushed up most by very expensive gas (+68 €/MWh): TTF is at 84.8 €/MWh, 58% above its 12-month average. It is also pushed up by weak wind (+32 €/MWh): wind farms are generating only 109 MW, against about 509 MW normally at this time of year.

Imports from Sweden use almost the full interconnector capacity (NordBalt – 689 of 700 MW): electricity in Sweden costs 190.1 €/MWh (18:00–19:00 – 224.1 €/MWh). The interconnector does not let more of the cheaper electricity through, so neighbouring markets raise the price (+32 €/MWh). For 23 hours the interconnectors from Latvia are fully loaded, so electricity in Lithuania is more expensive than in Latvia, by 66.4 €/MWh on average over the day.

The remaining part (“other”, −43 €/MWh) is not explained by these drivers. For almost the whole day the price is lower than the model expects: the interconnectors are not fully loaded and neighbouring prices do not stand out, so no clear cause is visible in the data.
